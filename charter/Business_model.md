
# Company Profile

## Industry
Vula Health Partners functions within the larger healthcare industry, providing digital referral coordination platforms connecting primary healthcare clinics with regional hospital specialists.

## Growth Pressures & Operational Assumptions
* **Clinic Scale & Volume (Assumption):** Vula assumes supporting a regional network of approximately 50 primary clinics, processing an estimated 500 daily digital mobile referrals across Western Cape districts.
* **Specialist Network (Assumption):** Operating with an assumed pool of roughly 120 on-call and resident specialist physicians spanning triage, obstetrics, orthopedics, and internal medicine.

## Infrastructure Deficits & Operational Challenges
* **Unreliable Mobile Data:** Primary clinic sites frequently encounter intermittent connectivity, low-bandwidth constraints, and unstable mobile data links[cite: 3].
* **Clinic Power Outages:** Load shedding and local electrical grid failures routinely disrupt clinic operations, requiring offline-resilient local caching for medical images and referral drafts[cite: 3].
* **Interoperability & Data Silos:** Most healthcare facilities rely on legacy paper files or fragmented electronic systems (e.g., DHIS2, localized clinic databases)[cite: 3]. Integrating digital mobile referrals into these disconnected systems requires heavy custom engineering[cite: 3].

## Regulatory & Liability Context
* **Compliance Scope:** Managing sensitive patient health records and medical imagery demands strict adherence to clinical privacy laws such as POPIA[cite: 3]. 
* **Data Processing Agreements (Assumption):** Vula assumes that partner hospitals and medical facilities will formally execute binding Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) before platform deployment.
