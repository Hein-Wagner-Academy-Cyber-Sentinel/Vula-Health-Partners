Charter

Scope: 
Vula Health Partners is designed to handle digital mobile referrals, Safely store sensitive patient images and medical special protected information, in compliance with POPIA and set up the core infrastructure. This includes establishing role-based access control clinic nurses and hospital specialists, as-well-as other relevant staff members that need access to medical information and are authorized to view it. We also are responsible for creating the needed secure data pipelines that connects fragmented clinic systems.

Success criteria:
We have achieved deploying a fully automated infrastructure  the pipelines run green on every commit. This ensures that all data is AES-256 encrypted, including patient records and all meta data. This also verifies that specialist users can access clinical files without exposing any of the other patient’s information.  

Assumptions:
We assume that hospital and medical facilities will agree to utilize our software and therefore execute the required and binding data processing agreements and that clinic and hospital staff will at minimum have access to basic mobile   data to initiate referrals. 

Out of scope:
Developing the front-end mobile application for the community health workers is not in our scope. Additionally, manually digitizing legacy paper files for integration into our systems and new data bases is strictly excluded from the prerogative and scope of Vula Health Partners and is the responsibility of the respective medical institutions, clinics and other relevant facilities.
