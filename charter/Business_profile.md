Company profile

Industry: Vula Health partners functions in the larger health industry 
Growth pressures
Infrastructure Defisets: Unreliable mobile data and clinic power outages
Regulatory & Liability Boundaries: Managing sensitive patient images and data demands strict compliance with privacy laws (such as POPIA or GDPR). 
Interoperability & Data Silos: Most facilities use legacy paper files or fragmented electronic systems (e.g., DHIS2, localized clinic databases). Integrating digital mobile referrals into these disconnected systems requires heavy custom engineering.

POPIA 
Data Classification

Patient Identifiable: Full names, IDs, dates of birth, sex, contact details, and location (Confidential).
Special Clinical Data (PHI / Health Records):** Medical histories (Restricted / Special Personal Information).
Healthcare Provider Data: Professional names and HPCSA registration numbers (Internal / Confidential).
Operational & System Metadata: Timestamps and tracking metrics (Internal/Restricted/POPIA Personal).


Regulatory compliance. 

Role: Responsible Partyfor direct platform operations and an authorized Operatorfor partner healthcare institutions, adhering strictly to the Protection of Personal Information Act.
Special Personal Information (Sections 26 & 32): Health data processing is restricted to authorized medical professionals and institutions exclusively for clinical triage, diagnosis, and care management.
Accountability & Governance (Condition 1): Overseen by a registered Information Officer with formal POPIA & PAIA Compliance Manuals.
Lawful Basis & Purpose Limitation (Conditions 2 & 3): Data collection is minimized, purpose-driven, and bound by clinical necessity, statutory mandates, or explicit consent. Adult clinical records adhere to the HPCSA minimum 6-year retention** guideline.
Processing Boundaries & Quality (Conditions 4 & 5): Prohibits secondary commercialization, marketing, or external analytics on identifiable patient data. Research requires irreversible anonymization, backed by systematic verification controls.
Technical & Physical Safeguards (Condition 7): Utilizes a Zero Trust framework, multi-factor authentication, role-based access control and AES-256 / TLS 1.3 encryption at rest and in transit.
Operator Agreements & Incident Response (Sections 21 & 22): Enforces binding Data Processing Agreements with third parties and maintains mandatory protocols for immediate breach notification to the Information Regulator and affected data subjects.
Cross-Border Transfers (Section 72):** Hosting and SaaS processing remain localized within South Africa or compliant offshore jurisdictions (e.g., GDPR-aligned regions) supported by robust corporate safeguards.
